# Economic Growth Models in Excel

Linked-formula spreadsheet models of long-run growth, from Malthusian stagnation to modern growth, plus a development-accounting study built on Penn World Table 11.0. Every parameter is a cell, so changing α, saving rates, depreciation or productivity growth updates each path and chart.

| Workbook | Model | What it shows |
|----------|-------|---------------|
| `malthusian-stagnation.xlsx` | Malthusian economy | Income, fertility and consumption paths converging to a stagnant steady state, and the response to a parameter shock |
| `malthus-to-solow-transition.xlsx` | Two-sector Malthus to Solow | Land-based agriculture alongside industry: productivity growth in each sector, wages, and the industry share over the transition |
| `unified-growth-theory.xlsx` | Unified growth theory | Escape from the Malthusian regime with education investment, fertility, land rents and per-capita income growth |
| `human-capital-augmented-solow.xlsx` | Solow model with physical and human capital | Capital, human capital and output paths, their growth rates, and how the saving rates sK and sH shift them |
| `development-accounting-pwt11.xlsx` | Development accounting | PWT 11.0 cross-country data split into capital, human capital and TFP contributions to income gaps |
| `growth-dynamic-programming-backward-induction.xlsx` | Finite-horizon optimal growth | Capital, consumption and output paths solved by backward induction |

Tools: Excel (linked formulas, charts), Penn World Table 11.0 (Feenstra, Inklaar & Timmer).
